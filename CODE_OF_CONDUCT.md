# Code of conduct

Quire follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and criticize code, not people. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are
not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

To report a problem, write to the maintainer, [@rickmff](https://github.com/rickmff), through a
[private report](https://github.com/rickmff/quire/security/advisories/new) or a direct message. Reports are kept
private. The maintainer may remove content, or block a person from the project, for a breach of this code.
